For over fifteen years, our institution has provided a warm, family-like second home for **children aged 1.5 to 6 years**. Both our creche and kindergarten groups are thoughtfully designed so little ones can explore the world at their own natural pace, without pressure.

We deeply believe that the cornerstone of early childhood is **unhurried, imaginative free play**. Our rooms feature natural wooden toys, cozy reading nooks, and creative workshops, guided by consistent, experienced educators who nurture curiosity and kindness.
